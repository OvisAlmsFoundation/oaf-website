Frequently Asked Questions

Is Ovis Alms Foundation a registered nonprofit organization, and are donations tax-deductible?

Yes. Ovis Alms Foundation is recognized by the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) as a tax-exempt charitable organization under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.

Donations to the foundation are generally tax-deductible for federal income tax purposes, to the extent permitted by law.

How can I request assistance through GraManna?

GraManna is currently under development and is not yet accepting requests for assistance.

If you are interested in receiving assistance, you can sign up for launch and availability notifications on our GraManna page.

Once GraManna becomes available in your area, eligible participants will be able to register and submit assistance requests through the application.

Signing up for notifications does not constitute an application for assistance or guarantee eligibility or aid.
