Future Expansion

The map is still being drawn.

The Foundation envisions GraManna eventually reaching communities throughout the United States, with the possibility of extending its charitable presence internationally.

The pace and scope of that expansion will depend on operational readiness, available resources, and the experience gained along the way. Additional locations and timelines have yet to be determined.
